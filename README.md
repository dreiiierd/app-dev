# My Favorite movie: *DETACHMENT*

## Description
*Detachment* is a psychological drama that follows Henry Barthes, a substitute teacher who drifts from school to school, avoiding emotional connections with students and colleagues alike. When he arrives at a struggling public high school filled with disillusioned teachers and neglected students, his quiet compassion and empathy begin to affect those around him — even as he battles his own inner grief and trauma.
